# Aurie campaign assets

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for the Aurie storefront redesign. These are editorial campaign visuals, not product catalogue photographs. Original uploaded product images, prices, stock, and saved shopping data are preserved. The legacy internal storage names remain for compatibility.

The generated files are saved in the project at:

- `frontend/public/images/aurie-hero.png` — transparent walking model layered over the live HTML wordmark.
- `frontend/public/images/aurie-editorial.png` — seasonal banner and menswear collection image.
- `frontend/public/images/aurie-women.png` — womenswear, draping detail, and atelier imagery.

## Final generation prompts

### Hero (transparent background enabled)

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent fashion campaign cutout for a black-and-white ecommerce hero. Full-length editorial studio photograph of one stylish adult Black male fashion model, side profile walking confidently toward the right, head slightly lowered, hands in pockets. Wearing a black baseball cap, oversized charcoal long trench coat open over a black shirt, loose black tailored trousers and white minimalist sneakers. A long natural mid-stride pose, coat slightly flowing. Entire figure from cap to both shoes visible with small clear margin, centered in a portrait 2:3 composition. Premium fashion photography, realistic fabric texture, high contrast entirely black-and-white grayscale. Soft directional studio lighting, sharp detailed subject. Genuinely transparent background with clean alpha edges. No background, no typography, no logos, no watermarks, no border, no collage. This will be layered in front of a huge AURIE wordmark in HTML.

### Seasonal editorial

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide luxury fashion editorial banner, 1536x1024 landscape. A handsome adult Black male fashion model in an oversized black open collar shirt and black jacket, waist-up portrait, looking thoughtfully to the left, textured short natural hair. Model occupies the RIGHT half, cut off by the bottom edge, head near top with a little margin; LEFT half is completely empty flat pale light gray seamless studio background for HTML headline. Realistic sophisticated fashion photography entirely black and white grayscale, natural skin and cotton textures, diffused studio light, soft shadows, crisp contemporary magazine aesthetic. Absolutely no text, logos, letters, watermark, borders or other people.

### Womenswear and atelier

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait fashion collection editorial image. Beautiful adult Black woman with hair in a sleek low bun wearing a flowing black sculptural one shoulder draped dress, elegant modest fashion editorial, framed from head to just below hips, composed in center, pale gray seamless studio background, premium black and white fashion photography with realistic silk textures, subtle confident expression, natural arms and hands, soft light and film grain. Vertical 2:3 image. No typography, no logo, no watermark. A campaign mood image for a contemporary Rwanda-rooted fashion brand.
